On September 30th, 2026, I moved from Madrid to Annecy (France). 

As you might have heard, the life of a postdoc typically involves a series of moves, before one can settle somewhere with a secure permanent position. Mine is no different: after Lisbon, London, Liverpool and Madrid, it has now come the time for a new city -- Annecy.

For my second postdoc, I joined the [Laboratoire d’Annecy de Physique Théorique (LAPTh)](https://www.lapth.cnrs.fr/en/). The LAPTh is a mixed research unit of [CNRS (Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique)](https://www.cnrs.fr/en) and [Savoie Mont Blanc University](https://www.univ-smb.fr/), and is located in the beautiful town of Annecy, with its gorgeous lake and mountain background.

As with every move, at the beginning everything is new: new job, new colleagues, new office, new appartment, new town... Then there's all the logistics, with new contracts (job, flat, electricity, water, internet), new phone numbers, new bank accounts. In some cases---like mine now---a new language as well. Although my portuguese roots, spanish experience and friend choices have given me a head start with the French language, I'm by no means fluent and will have to learn it properly (I'm currently taking classes at the USMB). 

Some things may be less obvious, but no less real. The supermarkets you liked, the coffee shops you regularly went to, your favourite restaurants, parks, museums, cinemas; all of these are gone. You're now relearning your shopping, looking for new favourites amongst the restaurants, cafés, boulangeries and the like. 

One adjusts. And one enjoys the perks of the new town. The new environment. The new people one meets and the new experiences one finds. And enjoy it we shall :) 

As for the the work, very little will change. I will keep exploring the connection between string theory and the clues the Universe gives us. That's the plan, but who knows where these new french winds will take us!
